## summary

- when a manual **Compact history** finds nothing to retell, a note with the numbers replaces the red error: how much history sits outside the summary and how much the tail takes. next to it is **Compact all but the newest messages** for chats where the tail covers the whole history
- the compaction settings are split into groups with icons, as in the app settings: General, This chat now, the compaction button, Auto compaction, Blocks, Prompts, Injection point. the auto-compaction lines show only while it is on
- "This chat now" is a bar and a few numbers instead of a paragraph: what the button would keep and what it would retell
- each prompt hint is one line, and the macro reference moved to its own row
- a new **Memory** section in the glossary: how the AI remembers a chat, summary, blocks, manual and auto compaction, merging and block size, summary prompts

## chat

- **the composer is not locked while a message is being edited**: typing, guidance, paste and attachments work as usual. only sending is blocked — it shows a toast and keeps the text in the field
- the send button turns into **Stop on the tap**, and stopping a generation, regeneration or continuation shows at once: a partial reply, a merged continuation or the restored pre-regen variation. while the write is up, edit, swipes and plugin writes do not go through
- **the counter between a message's variation arrows opens a list**: every swipe, nested sub-swipe and greeting with a short preview, the current one highlighted, and a jump straight to the picked one. error and cleaned variations are tagged
- quick taps on the arrows are no longer dropped: the controls are rebuilt only when their set changes
- **hidden characters** toggle on a triple tap of the "Characters" header (not on a folder, not while search is open). on desktop there is a hotkey, `Ctrl+Shift+H` by default, configurable in settings → desktop
- the chat no longer moves up when the composer grows on a long line: the list stays put and the composer grows over it. "Scroll With the Input Field" in the chat settings brings the old behaviour back
- "Auto-scroll While Streaming" in the chat settings: the chat does not follow a reply as it types. sending a message still jumps to the bottom

## lists

- Regex, lorebooks and personas move to the **presets list screen**: folders, filter, sorting, manual order by dragging, multi-select, adding from a button
- the lorebook editor opens inside the list's sheet instead of a full page. per-lorebook index management is gone from the editor — vectors live on the Vectors screen, the per-entry status text stays
- when adding a binding, characters and chats are picked from the **regular lists**: the card grid with search, favorites and filters, and the Dialogs list. a variation binding picks a concrete variation, and already-bound items are left out

## vectors

- a **standalone Vectors screen** in Tools, the sidebar and the chat drawer: the embedding preset, connection, lorebook search (threshold, top K, scan depth) and index rebuild in one place
- embedding settings leave the API screen — only LLM and Agents stay there
- "Recalling memory…" no longer waits behind background indexing: requests from the chat go first, indexing waits

## generation

- Gemini 4 honours the reasoning effort setting (`low`, `medium`, `high`), and Gemini 3.7/3.8 Flash and 3.1 Pro get the levels they actually support
- a reply timeout says how many seconds the server sent nothing instead of "took too long"; an error card no longer keeps a frozen clock

## pictures and ExtBlocks

- a second **Regenerate image** tap no longer drops a finished picture: redraws of one block run one at a time, and the button goes dark after the first tap
- if the provider refuses, the finished picture stays and the error comes as a toast
- pictures in `render: panel` panels are no longer shown as broken

## windows

- the setup warns when the system Visual C++ runtime is too old and offers to download a new one or continue; the needed runtime ships with the app
- a fresh install lets you pick a folder — Glaze makes its own folder inside it. updates stay in the picked folder

## backup

- a backup made by a **newer app version** restores with a warning: data this version does not know about will be lost

## fixes

- a branch of a hand-made variation stays on that variation and creates no new card
- hiding a selection applies at once, no longer flips already hidden messages back and reaches messages outside the scroll window
- a regenerate over an error replaces it, and the error stays with its own variation without picking up another variation's sub-swipes
- the keyboard no longer lifts the composer off the screen; the scroll buttons and the context coverage card are drawn in the page itself and stay out of the fullscreen composer
- on iOS and macOS a notification no longer takes the avatar out of the library: character pictures no longer disappear after a restart
- the JanitorAI catalog on Windows no longer crashes the app while reading cookies
- pinning the cache anchor on a message retold in a summary explains why and points at deleting the covering block
- edits, deletes, swipes and Stop no longer stop working after a chat rebuild — the editor says when a save did not go through
